## Corpus-wide invariants (checked per fixture by the harness, SPEC-005)

1. Every classification is three-state with ≥1 reason; `not-affected` only with positive evidence.
2. Every wave prerequisite is phrased "stated, not verified live" where it names a publication/restore fact.
3. Every `ProducerClaim`-derived entry cites `producer-evidence.v0`; nothing derived from names.
4. Contradictions produce provisional waves; gaps produce `unknown`; neither is ever dropped.
5. Goldens validate against `plan.schema.v1.json` (now with required ownership/actionType/evidenceKinds/confidence, external⇒request-await pairing, provisional⇒blockedOn, conditional⇒condition, stop⇒empty-waves, CYCLE_DETECTED⇒cyclePath).
6. An observed consumer edge is never downgraded by coverage gaps; a producer repo's zero references never prove its packages' independence (SPEC-001 §4 fusion rules).
